What accompanying material makes accessible in a fixed recording, through Tom Zé's Com Defeito de Fabricação (Fabrication Defect) and Björk's Biophilia

Accompanying material can do more than tell a listener what kind of record they hold. It can change the routes by which they reach what the recording contains.

Where the recording stays fixed, accompanying material does not alter the signal. It can alter the cues through which a listener reaches features of that signal.

An exploratory comparative essay on linguistic architecture and the accessibility of mathematics

Mathematical relationships do not change across languages, but the cost of reaching them may.

The short answer: languages do not change what is mathematically true, nor who can do mathematics. They change where the effort falls. Some steps are made easier, others harder, and the effects are narrower than popular accounts suggest.

A Method for Making Epistemic Architectures Visible

Why do people examining the same evidence reach incompatible conclusions?

The IRIS method does not resolve disagreement. It makes the architecture of disagreement visible — enabling disputants to identify not merely what they disagree about but which processing rules generate the disagreement.
